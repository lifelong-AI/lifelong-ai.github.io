About

I am a research assistant at the Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University. My research interests lie in multimodal reasoning, agent safety, and human-centered intelligent systems.

My recent work focuses on multimodal environment understanding and smart-home multimodal prompt injection. I use controlled experiments and representation interventions to study decision reliability in multimodal models, and evaluate single- and multi-agent systems under prompt injection across visual, speech, and audio-video inputs.

Previously, I received my master's and bachelor's degrees in Transportation Engineering from Chang'an University, where my research focused primarily on deep learning.

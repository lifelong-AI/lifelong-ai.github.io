About

I am a research assistant at the Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University. My research focuses on multimodal understanding and reasoning, particularly how AI models interpret their environments and use that information to make decisions.

I am interested in how these models represent the world, how they reason in unfamiliar situations, and what makes their decisions reliable. My goal is to build AI that genuinely understands the world and can use that understanding to act in real-world environments.

I received my master's and bachelor's degrees in Transportation Engineering from Chang'an University. There, I worked on deep learning for time-series modeling and human-state prediction.

Personal Project

PBG-MTFNet

EEG-based fatigue detection

I designed and implemented a deep learning model to detect fatigue from EEG signals, bringing together spatial relationships between electrodes, frequency-band information, and temporal dynamics.

Model design
Per-band graph convolution captures relationships between electrodes within each frequency band. Multi-scale temporal-frequency modeling combines information across bands and time.
Evaluation
I evaluated the model on SEED-VIG and SADT under mixed-subject and leave-one-subject-out settings, examining both detection performance and generalization to unseen subjects.

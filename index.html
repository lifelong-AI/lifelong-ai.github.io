About me

My research focuses on multimodal perception and understanding, particularly how models integrate visual, auditory, and other signals to interpret real-world scenes and events. I am currently a researcher at the Future Laboratory, Tsinghua University.

I am interested in the representations and reasoning processes that support this ability, including how models use context to identify relevant evidence and interpret ambiguous situations. I also study how these capabilities generalize across tasks and settings.

My previous research focused on deep learning and time-series modeling.

Selected Projects

Physiological signal modeling

PBG-MTFNet

EEG-based fatigue detection

PBG-MTFNet brings together spatial relationships between electrodes, frequency-band information, and temporal dynamics to detect fatigue from EEG signals. Evaluation on SEED-VIG and SADT examines both detection performance and generalization to unseen subjects.

Model design
Per-band graph convolution captures relationships between electrodes within each frequency band. Multi-scale temporal-frequency modeling combines information across bands and time.
Evaluation
Experiments on SEED-VIG and SADT use mixed-subject and leave-one-subject-out settings to examine detection performance and generalization to unseen subjects.
Robotics & embodied learning

Truman: A Simulated Robot Learning Platform

MuJoCo simulation · autonomous exploration · 2D LiDAR SLAM · simulated touch

Truman’s World is a local MuJoCo platform for embodied robotics experiments. The 3D environment models robot and object geometry, mass, contact, and friction, making it possible to study how physical conditions affect interaction. For exploration, a differential-drive robot combines wheel odometry and IMU estimates with 2D LiDAR scan matching to build occupancy-grid maps. A separate Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) pipeline trains a policy to push a ball into a target zone. To test robustness, training varies ball mass, surface friction, motor strength, and command delay. Simulated tactile sensing records contact force and motion. The learned policy remains under evaluation and has not been transferred to physical hardware.

Room navigation
A 2D LiDAR SLAM pipeline combines wheel odometry, IMU measurements, scan matching, and occupancy-grid mapping for planar navigation in the 3D simulation.
Learning setup
The separate SAC pushing task varies simulated object and contact conditions. Training checkpoints and evaluation records are stored locally.
